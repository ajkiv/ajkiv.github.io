Farm Energy Management System

An AI-assisted dashboard that combines a farm's electricity metering, CHP unit output, weather, and price forecasts into one platform — giving operators plain-language recommendations for optimizing energy use.

View code on GitHub → Open live demo ↗

My contribution

I built the backend integration that connects to the farm's electricity system and polls live SuomiConnect metering data from it — how much electricity the farm is producing, selling, consuming, and buying — feeding those readings into the data pipeline the rest of the dashboard runs on.

Project & credits

Developed by the DAME project (South Savo Data Economy Accelerator), South-Eastern Finland University of Applied Sciences — Digitalia, in partnership with Harjun Maatila Oy, SuomiConnect Oy, and ProAgria Ry. Co-funded by the European Union via the European Regional Development Fund.
