Finnish Traffic Law Chatbot

A Finnish-language chatbot that answers questions about traffic offences using the actual text of the law. It finds the most relevant sections of the Criminal Code (Rikoslaki, chapter 23) and gives them to an LLM, so the answer is grounded in the statute instead of guessed from memory.

How it works

The law text is split into individual sections (§), and each section is turned into an embedding with a local multilingual model. When you ask a question, it is embedded the same way, and FAISS retrieves the three most relevant sections. These are passed to an OpenAI model, which writes the answer in Finnish.

Transparent by design

The interface shows three things: the generated answer, the legal sources used, and the full retrieved passages. This makes it easy to check the answer against the law and shows exactly how retrieval shapes the response. The model is instructed to separate statutory text from explanation and to say when the material isn't enough.

Built with

Python, Gradio, Sentence Transformers (paraphrase-multilingual-MiniLM-L12-v2), FAISS, OpenAI API

Scope and next steps

This is a deliberately small v1: sections 1–12 only, with no conversation memory, reranking or similarity threshold. Testing with real questions will show where retrieval or generation fails, and those findings will guide v2. It is a demonstration, not legal advice.
