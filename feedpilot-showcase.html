FeedPilot

A browser-based content discovery tool. Submit an RSS feed that reflects your interests, and FeedPilot analyzes it, finds feeds with similar interests, and surfaces new content suggestions you likely haven't seen — without a commercial platform's algorithm deciding for you.

My contributions

I was responsible for two parts of this project:

  • Feed recommendation algorithm — the partner's original implementation wasn't working as intended; I diagnosed and fixed it so it correctly recommends related content based on a submitted feed.
  • Data scraper — built the component that pulls in the open RSS feed data the recommendation algorithm runs on.
